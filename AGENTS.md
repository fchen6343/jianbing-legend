# Development priorities / 开发优先级

Read `docs/QUALITY_GATES.md` before implementing features. It records the user's latest priority and supersedes conflicting sequencing/shortcuts in the original reference `docs/MASTER_PLAN.zh-CN.md`.
开发前阅读 `docs/QUALITY_GATES.md`；最新体验优先原则优先于原始总指令中的冲突排期。

- Core pleasure is manual batter spreading, cracking/spreading eggs, multi-step folding and tangible equipment automation. Do not replace them with progress counters, one-click completion or decorative animations to meet content targets.
  核心乐趣是亲手摊糊、打蛋、分步折叠和真实设备自动化。不得用计数、一步按钮或装饰动画冒充完成。
- Fifty-day content may ship in stages. Every stage must build, run, complete real orders, preserve existing progress and include regression tests plus an honest playable acceptance record.
  五十天内容可以分阶段交付；每阶段必须可编译、运行、完整交餐、保留进度、有回归测试和真实试玩记录。
- Animation does not establish control quality; passing automated tests does not establish enjoyment. Mark manual acceptance as pending when it has not happened.
  动画不等于操作手感，自动测试通过不等于好玩；没有实际验收的项目必须标为待验收。
- Automation must be visible, optional and reversible during preparation. Compare manual/off/on behavior and retain player choices, key ingredients and interactions.
  自动化必须有可见工作过程、营业前可开关、可回到手动；验收关闭/开启差异，保留关键选择和互动。
- Do not mark the full game complete until the original specification AND current quality gates pass. Keep bilingual controls, original cartoon art, mode switching, restock and snack-claw interactions working.
  总需求和质量门槛全部达标前不得标为完整版；保留双语、原创卡通、模式切换、补货和伸缩夹子。
